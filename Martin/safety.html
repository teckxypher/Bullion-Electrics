Basic guidance for reducing electrical risk around homes and workplaces.

⚠

Do not improvise

Do not bypass breakers, use damaged cables or make temporary live connections.

⛔

Keep clear

Keep water away from electrical equipment and keep children away from exposed wiring.

⚡

Shocks

If someone is being shocked, isolate power if safe. Do not touch them while they remain connected.

✓

Call a professional

Use qualified electrical professionals for installations, repairs and testing.
